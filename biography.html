Biography

I'm Mudasir Khan — Tuan Muda. I'm a Malaysian-American author, columnist, data-map maker and builder. I've spent 15+ years running stores and operations at Walgreens, The Home Depot and Enterprise, and I ran my own Amazon delivery business.

I write about Malaysian and Singapore politics, the history of Chinese and Malay populations, and land, debt and who really owns a nation (Mortgaged Nation). I tell the data through maps.
