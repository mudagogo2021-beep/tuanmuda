Assumptions

What this proposal depends on.

An independent proposal by Mudasir Khan for local ownership of Cybercab branches. It is not affiliated with or approved by Tesla, Inc. Cybercab and Robotaxi are descriptive names. No branch is open, no vehicle has been supplied, and no guest can book a trip here.

Conditions

  • Cybercab supply is a written path, still to be agreed.
  • Operating permission, insurance, premises, and financing have to be in place before the first branch opens.
  • Seven weeks is a conditional target for that first branch and the start of its fill toward 200. It is not a national build-out. If a condition slips, the calendar moves.
  • The owner grant is proposed. It would give a first-time owner equity, control, and a share of profit. It is not funded, not a loan, and not a guaranteed income. Amount and cohort size wait on a term sheet, after legal, tax, and franchise review.
  • Settlement — how a branch is paid — is part of the same partnership conversation.
  • Easing traffic is a hypothesis. A pilot would measure total miles, including empty repositioning, peak load, and occupancy. Empty miles and induced demand can move the result the wrong way. No cure is claimed.
  • This site does not collect personal information or accept applications.

Where the network would grow

The proposed first markets are the Hartford-area suburbs in Connecticut and suburban New Jersey. Each branch reaches 200 Cybercabs on site before another opens. No town or property is selected.

Planning example

Wash turns are bays times operating minutes divided by minutes per wash. Charge sessions are stalls times operating minutes divided by charge minutes plus handling. The daily ceiling is the smallest of parking, wash turns, and charge sessions. With the example inputs that ceiling is 57.6, set by the wash bays. Editing the numbers on the daily-work section does not create a forecast.
